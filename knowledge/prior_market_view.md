# Prior Market View — Industrial Autonomy and AI

Source: Group Strategy market assessment, 2025-Q1 planning cycle. **This is the
group's last formally adopted position on this market.** It has not been
revisited since adoption and remains the basis of current segment plans.

---

**[PRIOR-01] Headline position.**
"Industrial AI adoption will remain concentrated in single-asset use cases
through the end of the decade. Meridian's leading position in predictive
maintenance and vision inspection covers the segments where budget actually
exists."

**[PRIOR-02] On fleet-level autonomy.**
"Coordinating fleets of heterogeneous machines is an unsolved research problem
and, more importantly, an unbudgeted one. Plant managers hold cell-level
budgets. We assess a fleet-level buying centre as a 2030-plus development and
recommend no capability investment in this planning cycle."

**[PRIOR-03] On the Nexara opportunity.**
"Reviewed and passed. Technically credible, commercially premature. We expect
this company to struggle to raise a subsequent round without a named
production customer."

**[PRIOR-04] On robot foundation models.**
"Demonstrations are impressive and irrelevant to industrial buyers, who require
determinism and certifiability. We see no path to a production deployment in a
regulated manufacturing environment within the planning horizon."

**[PRIOR-05] On agentic operations tooling.**
"A category in search of a problem. Plant operators do not want a chat
interface, they want fewer alarms. We recommend no action."

**[PRIOR-06] On the durability of the integration advantage.**
"Our commissioning organisation is not replicable by software-first entrants
and remains the group's most reliable competitive moat in automation."
