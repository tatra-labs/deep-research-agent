# Win/Loss Interview Extracts — FY26

Source: Group Strategy win/loss programme. Verbatim buyer statements, lightly
redacted for commercial confidentiality. Each extract carries a stable ID and
is quotable as evidence.

---

**[WL-01]** — Lost, D-2217, Loire Aerospace, $1.12M ARR, SEG-FLEET-AUTONOMY

> "Meridian gave us the best individual cell in the evaluation. That wasn't the
> question we were asking. We're putting in 40 cells over three years and we
> need one thing that schedules across all of them. When we asked for the fleet
> roadmap we got a slide that said 'future'. The other vendor showed us a
> working scheduler running 60 units."

**[WL-02]** — Lost, D-2211, Guangdong Assembly, $935k ARR, SEG-FLEET-AUTONOMY

> "Your engineers were the best in the room. That is genuinely true. But we are
> not buying engineers any more, we are buying software that means we need
> fewer of them."

**[WL-03]** — Lost, D-2230, Pilbara Ore, $1.34M ARR, SEG-FLEET-AUTONOMY

> "The procurement decision came down to who owned the orchestration layer.
> Meridian was reselling somebody else's. We'd rather buy from the company that
> writes it."

**[WL-04]** — Won, D-2218, Puget Semiconductor, $830k ARR, SEG-VISION-QA

> "Nobody else could commission this in fourteen weeks. That's the whole reason
> you won. Your inspection accuracy was second-best and it didn't matter."

**[WL-05]** — Lost, D-2205, Rhine Chemical, $890k ARR, SEG-INDUSTRIAL-DATA

> "We asked both vendors where our process data would live. One of them had an
> answer. Meridian's answer depended on the control-system vendor, and that
> vendor is not neutral about the question."

**[WL-06]** — Lost, D-2215, Osaka Fabrication, $344k ARR, SEG-AGENTIC-OPS

> "Our shift supervisors are already using an AI assistant that one of them
> found and paid for on a card. We asked Meridian for the equivalent and the
> answer was a professional-services quote."

**[WL-07]** — Won, D-2241, Nevada Battery, $845k ARR, SEG-PREDICTIVE-MAINT

> "The maintenance models are demonstrably better than anything else we tested,
> and you had reference sites we could call. This one was easy."

**[WL-08]** — Lost, D-2224, Shenzhen Modular, $760k ARR, SEG-PHYSICAL-AI

> "You asked us to specify the task and you'd program it. They asked us to show
> the task once. That is a different product category, and it's the one our
> board has heard of."
